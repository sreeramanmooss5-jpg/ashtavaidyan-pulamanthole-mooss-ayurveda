## 1. The Ashtavaidya Science of *Avarti* (101-Cycle Potentiation)
The technique of **Avarti**—repeatedly processing a medicated *Tailam* 7, 14, 21, or 101 times with fresh batches of herbal decoction (*Kwatha*), milk (*Ksheera*), and herbal paste (*Kalka*)—is one of the hallmark pharmaceutical contributions of Kerala's **Ashtavaidya** tradition.
- **Why 101 Cycles Matter:** Each cycle concentrates both lipophilic and amphiphilic phytonutrients while refining molecular dispersion, allowing **Dhanwantharam Avarti 101** to act in drop-level doses where ordinary oils require large volumes.
- **Clinical Administration Modes (Physician-Supervised Only):**
  - *Nasya Karma* (nasal drop instillation in cervical spondylosis, facial palsy / *Ardita*, and neurological disorders)
  - *Shiro Pichu* / *Thalam* (cranial retention)
  - Internal *Bindu* (drop) administration mixed with warm *Kashayam* or milk as prescribed by an Ayurveda physician.
