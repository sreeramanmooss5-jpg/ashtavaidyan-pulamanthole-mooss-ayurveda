## 1. Formulation Science & Traditional Applications
**Chemparathyadi Keram** (*Sahasrayogam* classical reference) is a traditional Kerala medicated coconut oil (*Kera Tailam*) prepared via multi-stage *Sneha Paka* using fresh juices (*Swarasa*) of **Chemparathi** (*Hibiscus rosa-sinensis*), **Vilwa** (*Aegle marmelos*), **Paranti** (*Ixora coccinea*), **Thulasi**, and **Neeli** (*Indigofera tinctoria*) with aromatic cooling spices in pure coconut oil.
- **Traditional Applications:** Widely used in Kerala pediatric (*Kaumarabhritya*) and dermatological practice for soothing scalp itching, dandruff, cradle cap, and childhood skin irritations (*Karappan* / eczema tendencies), as well as cooling *Pitta–Kapha* scalp conditions.
- **Application Mode:** For external scalp and skin application prior to bathing, as directed by an Ayurveda physician.
