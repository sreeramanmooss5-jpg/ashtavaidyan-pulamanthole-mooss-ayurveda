## 1. Formulation Overview & Verified Traditional Indications
**Agnimukhee Choornam** is a classical Ayurvedic herbal powder prepared at Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Mooss Pharmaceuticals under strict GMP quality controls (GMP Cert No. 575). As its name (*Agnimukhee* — kindling digestive fire) indicates, it is traditionally formulated to ignite sluggish *Jatharagni*, clear unassimilated metabolic residue (*Ama*), and restore downward flow of *Vata* (*Vatanulomana*).
