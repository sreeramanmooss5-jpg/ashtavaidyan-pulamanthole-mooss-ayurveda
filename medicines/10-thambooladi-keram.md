## 1. Formulation Background & Traditional Uses
**Thambooladi Keram** is a traditional Kerala medicated coconut oil formulated with **Thamboola** (*Piper betle* leaf extract) and warming, *Kapha–Vata* balancing botanicals processed via *Sneha Paka*.
- **Traditional Applications:** Used in Kerala households and pediatric/general OP practice for external scalp application in individuals prone to seasonal *Kapha* congestion, recurrent head colds, and scalp chilliness when switching hair oils.
- **Usage Guidance:** Apply externally to the crown (*Thalam*) or scalp before bathing with lukewarm water, as advised by an Ayurveda physician.
