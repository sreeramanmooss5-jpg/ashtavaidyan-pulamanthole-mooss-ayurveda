## 1. Classical *Bhaishajya Ratnavali* Reference & *Sandhana Kalpana*
**Dasamoolarishtam** is one of the cornerstone self-generated fermented formulations (*Arishtams*) of classical Ayurveda, referenced in *Bhaishajya Ratnavali* (verified on bottle label asset `03-8.jpg`). Combining the ten roots (**Dasamoola** — *Bilva, Agnimantha, Shyonaka, Patala, Gambhari, Brihati, Kantakari, Gokshura, Shalaparni, Prishniparni*) with *Chitraka*, *Pushkaramoola*, *Guduchi*, *Amalaki*, *Draksha*, and *Dhataki Pushpa*, natural fermentation extracts both water-soluble and hydro-alcoholic phytonutrients for rapid absorption.
- **Traditional Clinical Applications:** Widely prescribed by Ayurveda physicians for convalescence after fever or illness, digestive weakness (*Agnimandya*), respiratory stamina, *Vata* pacification, and classical **postnatal recovery (*Sutika Paricharya*)**.
- **Usage Note:** Administered after meals in physician-prescribed dosage.
