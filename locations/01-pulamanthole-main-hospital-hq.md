## 1. The Ancestral Ashtavaidya Seat Along the Kunthi River
Situated beside the serene Kunthi River (a tributary of the Nila / Bharathapuzha) in **Pulamanthole**, our main campus combines the ancestral **Pulamanthole Mana** heritage (Est. 1795, authors of the *Kairali* commentary on Vagbhata's *Ashtanga Hridaya*) with a **ROHINI Registration Certified Hospital** and our **GMP-Certified Ayurvedic Medicine Manufacturing unit (GMP Cert No. 575)**.

## 2. On-Campus Clinical Divisions & Facilities
- **24/7 Inpatient Panchakarma & Rehabilitation Wards:** Economy AC/Non-AC rooms and Premium riverside suites for multi-week inpatient *Shodhana* and neuro-musculoskeletal recovery.
- **All 8 Specialist OP/IP Clinics:** [PMIC Inherited Clinic](../clinics/01-pmic-inherited-clinic.md), [Panchakarma](../clinics/02-panchakarma-detoxification-clinic.md), [Arthritis & Joint Disorders](../clinics/03-arthritis-joint-disorders-clinic.md), [Back Pain & Spine Care](../clinics/04-back-pain-spine-care-clinic.md), [Migraine Clinic](../clinics/05-migraine-headache-clinic.md), [Women's Health](../clinics/06-womens-health-prasoothi-tantra-clinic.md), [Post-Stroke Rehabilitation](../clinics/07-brain-neurological-stroke-rehabilitation-clinic.md), and [Pediatric Ayurveda](../clinics/08-pediatric-ayurveda-kaumarabhritya-clinic.md).
- **In-House GMP Pharmacy & Quality Lab:** Freshly prepared *Kashayams*, *Arishtams*, *Choornams*, *Kerams*, *Tailams*, and *Avarti* formulations dispensed directly to inpatient therapy theatres and outstation patients.

## 3. How to Reach Pulamanthole Campus
- **By Rail:** 10 km from **Pattambi Railway Station (`PTB`)** and 24 km from **Shornur Junction (`SRR`)** (major all-India rail hub connecting Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, and Mangaluru).
- **By Air:** 55 km from **Calicut International Airport (`CCJ`)** and 95 km from **Coimbatore International Airport (`CJB`)**.
