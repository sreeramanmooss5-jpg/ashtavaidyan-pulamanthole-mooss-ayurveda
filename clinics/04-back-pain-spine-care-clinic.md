## 1. Root-Cause Biomechanical & *Apana Vata* Spine Protocol
Chronic lower back pain, intervertebral disc prolapse (IVDP), cervical spondylosis, and sciatica (*Gridhrasi*) rarely resolve with analgesics alone because nerve root irritation is sustained by paraspinal muscle spasm, intervertebral dehydration, and *Apana Vata* stagnation in the pelvic/lumbar region (*Kati Pradesha*).

### Classical Differentiation of *Gridhrasi* (Sciatica)
1. ***Vataja Gridhrasi*:** Sharp, radiating lancinating pain from the lumbar spine through the gluteal region, posterior thigh, calf, and foot, with paresthesia (*Toda*, *Spandana*).
2. ***Vata-Kaphaja Gridhrasi*:** Radiating pain accompanied by deep heaviness (*Gaurava*), coldness, morning stiffness, and sluggish digestion (*Agnimandya*).

### Structured 3-Phase Spine Rehabilitation
- **Phase 1 (De-Spasm & Anti-Inflammatory):** *Patra Potala Sweda (Elakizhi)*, *Choorna Pinda Sweda (Podikizhi)*, and localized *Kati Vasti* / *Greeva Vasti* / *Prishta Vasti* using warm medicated Tailams prepared under GMP Cert No. 575.
- **Phase 2 (*Apana Vata* & Disc Nourishment):** Course of classical *Erandamooladi Niruha Vasti* and *Anuvasana / Matra Vasti* to decompress lumbar nerve roots and restore pelvic-lumbar *Vata* flow.
- **Phase 3 (Core Strengthening & Ergonomic Reset):** *Njavarakizhi* paraspinal nourishment and customized posture guidelines for desk and travel professionals.
