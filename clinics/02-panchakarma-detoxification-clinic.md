## 1. Clinical Overview & Ashtavaidya Panchakarma Protocol
At Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Mooss Ayurveda Hospital, **Panchakarma** is practiced strictly as a physician-supervised clinical intervention—never a generic spa package. Rooted in the *Kairali* commentary on Vagbhata's *Ashtanga Hridaya*, every inpatient program is preceded by an individual *Prakriti–Vikriti* and *Agni* assessment and divided into three classical phases:
1. **Purvakarma (Preparatory Phase):** *Deepana–Pachana* (digestive priming), internal *Snehapana* (measured medicated ghee/oil administration), and external *Abhyanga* + *Swedana* to mobilize deep-tissue *Doshas* toward the gastrointestinal tract (*Koshtha*).
2. **Pradhanakarma (Core Shodhana Phase):** Selection of the appropriate classical elimination therapy—*Vamana*, *Virechana* (purgation), *Niruha & Anuvasana Vasti* (medicated enema protocols), *Nasya* (nasal administration), or *Raktamokshana*—alongside Kerala specialty procedures (*Kayaseka / Pizhichil*, *Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda / Njavarakizhi*, *Patra Potala Sweda / Elakizhi*, *Takradhara*, and *Shirodhara*) using freshly prepared formulations from our GMP-certified pharmacy (GMP Cert No. 575).
3. **Paschatkarma (Rejuvenation & Diet Reset):** Graduated *Samsarjana Krama* diet progression to rebuild *Jatharagni*, followed by targeted *Rasayana* formulations.
