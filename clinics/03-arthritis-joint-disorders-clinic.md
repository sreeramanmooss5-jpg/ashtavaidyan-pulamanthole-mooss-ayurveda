## 1. Clinical Differentiation: Why Inflammatory and Degenerative Joint Pain Require Opposite First Steps
A critical diagnostic rule at our **Arthritis & Joint Disorders Clinic** is distinguishing between **inflammatory, *Ama*-driven arthritis (*Amavata* / *Vatarakta*)** and **degenerative, *Vata*-driven joint wear (*Sandhigata Vata*)**:
- **Stage 1 — *Ama* Dominant (Rheumatoid Arthritis, Acute Gout, Morning Stiffness & Swelling):** Applying heavy medicated oils prematurely onto an *Ama*-swollen joint traps inflammation. Treatment begins with *Langhana*, *Deepana–Pachana*, dry/warm fomentation (*Ruksha Sweda*, *Choorna Pinda Sweda*, *Dhanyamla Dhara*), and gut-clearing internal *Kashayams*.
- **Stage 2 — *Nirama Vata* / *Dhatu Kshaya* (Osteoarthritis of the Knee, Cartilage Wear, Crepitus):** Once *Ama* is cleared, therapy shifts to deep lipid nourishment (*Bruhmana* & *Snehana*) using classical [Dhanwantharam Thailam](../medicines/11-dhanwantharam-thailam.md), [Dhanwantharam Avarti 101](../medicines/12-dhanwantharam-avarti-101.md), *Janu Vasti*, *Upanaha Sweda* (medicated poultice), and *Tikta Ksheera Vasti* (medicated milk-decoction enemas scientifically recognized in classical texts for bone and cartilage nourishment, *Asthi–Majja Dhatu*).
