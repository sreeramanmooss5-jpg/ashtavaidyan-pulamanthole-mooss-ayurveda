## 1. Clinical Overview & Classical *Kaumarabhritya* Philosophy
The **Pulamanthole Mooss Inherited Clinic (PMIC)** is a specialized pediatric and neurodevelopmental division of Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Mooss Ayurveda Hospital, rooted in classical Vagbhata (*Ashtanga Hridaya*) and *Kaumarabhritya* principles. Rather than offering short, generalized wellness cycles or making unverified miracle claims, PMIC operates a structured, long-duration, family-centric care model focused on improving digestive assimilation (*Jatharagni*), gut–brain signaling (*Agni–Srotas–Ojas*), sensory-motor regulation, sleep quality, and functional independence.

## 2. Conditions Supported at PMIC
- **Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD):** Supporting sensory regulation, eye contact, sleep architecture, and digestive comfort alongside behavioral and speech therapy.
- **Cerebral Palsy (CP):** Individualized *Snehana*, *Swedana*, *Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda (Njavarakizhi)*, and *Matra Vasti* protocols to support muscle tone (*Mamsa Dhatu*), spasticity reduction, and motor milestones.
- **Global Developmental Delay (GDD) & Speech Delay:** Addressing *Udana Vata* and *Prana Vata* coordination alongside gut *Ama* clearance.
- **ADHD & Childhood Hyperactivity:** Non-sedating Ayurvedic neuro-sensory calming (*Shiro Pichu*, *Thalapothichil*, *Takradhara*) and dietary regulation.
- **Learning Disabilities & Inherited / Genetic Syndromes:** Long-term supportive physiological care and parental diet/routine counseling.
- **Prenatal, Natal & Postnatal Care:** Classical *Garbhini Paricharya* and *Sutika Paricharya* in collaboration with Dr. Rosni K. (BAMS, MS Ayu).

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Block)
**Q1: How does Ayurveda approach speech delay and Autism Spectrum Disorder at PMIC?**  
At PMIC, evaluation begins with the child's digestive fire (*Jatharagni*), bowel regularity, and sleep rhythm. In classical Ayurveda, unassimilated metabolic residue (*Ama*) obstructs micro-channels (*Manovaha Srotas*) and disturbs *Prana Vata* (sensory processing) and *Udana Vata* (speech expression). Our 3-stage protocol combines gentle gut correction, child-friendly external cranial/body therapies, and *Medhya* nourishment alongside the child's ongoing speech and occupational therapies.
