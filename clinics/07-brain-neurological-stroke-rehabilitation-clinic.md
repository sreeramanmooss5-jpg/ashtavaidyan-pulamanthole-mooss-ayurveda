## 1. Collaborative Post-Stroke (*Pakshaghata*) & Neuro-Rehabilitation
Once acute emergency neurological stabilization is completed at a modern stroke unit, the sub-acute neuroplasticity window (the first 3 to 12 months post-stroke) is decisive for recovering motor control, gait stability, speech clarity, and daily independence. In classical Vagbhata texts, hemiplegia/hemiparesis (*Pakshaghata*) is caused by severe *Vata Prakopa* drying the *Sira* (vascular pathways) and *Snayu* (tendons/nerves) on one side of the body.

## 2. Sequential Inpatient Rehabilitation Protocol
1. **Snehana & Srotas Softening:** Gentle external oleation with [Dhanwantharam Thailam](../medicines/11-dhanwantharam-thailam.md) and [Dhanwantharam Avarti 101](../medicines/12-dhanwantharam-avarti-101.md) combined with internal Vata-pacifying formulations (such as [Agnimukhee Choornam](../medicines/01-agnimukhee-choornam.md) and classical *Kashayams* alongside the patient's neurologist-prescribed medications).
2. **Kayaseka (*Pizhichil*) & *Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda (Njavarakizhi)*:** Synchronized warm medicated oil stream therapy and medicated rice-bolus fomentation to reduce post-stroke muscle spasticity, prevent joint contractures, and stimulate peripheral neuromuscular pathways.
3. **Matra Vasti, Nasya & Physiotherapy Integration:** Targeted *Vasti* and *Nasya* protocols paired with daily assisted gait and occupational rehabilitation.
