## 1. Dignified, Evidence-Conscious Ayurvedic Gynecology & Maternal Care
Led by **Dr. Rosni K. (BAMS, MS Ayu)**, the **Women's Health Clinic** provides specialized outpatient and inpatient care across every stage of a woman's life—from adolescent menstrual regulation to fertility preparation, classical postnatal recovery (*Sutika Paricharya*), and perimenopausal transition.

## 2. Core Clinical Programs
- **Menstrual & Endocrine Balance (*Artava Dushti*):** Root-cause metabolic and pelvic *Apana Vata* regulation for PCOS, irregular cycles, dysmenorrhea (*Kashtartava*), endometriosis support, and heavy menstrual bleeding (*Asrigdara*).
- **Pre-Conception & Fertility Support (*Garbhadhana Samskara*):** *Shodhana* (detoxification), *Uttara Vasti*, and *Rasayana* protocols for couples preparing for conception.
- **Classical Kerala Postnatal Care (*Sutika Paricharya*):** Structured postpartum recovery utilizing [Dhanwantharam Thailam](../medicines/11-dhanwantharam-thailam.md) *Abhyanga*, medicated herbal bath (*Vethukuli*), abdominal binding, lactation nourishment, and [Dasamoolarishtam](../medicines/04-dasamoolarishtam.md) to restore pelvic floor tone, prevent chronic backache, and stabilize postpartum *Vata*.
- **Perimenopause & Bone/Skin Healthspan (Vedic Bloom Synergy):** Managing hot flashes, sleep disturbance, bone density, and skin vitality.
