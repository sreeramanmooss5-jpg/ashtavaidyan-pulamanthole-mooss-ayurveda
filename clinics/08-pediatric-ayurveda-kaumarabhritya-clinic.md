## 1. Gentle, Age-Appropriate Classical Pediatric Care
Distinct from the long-duration neurodevelopmental programs at [PMIC](01-pmic-inherited-clinic.md), our **Pediatric Ayurveda & Child Care Clinic** addresses everyday childhood immunity (*Vyadhikshamatva*), digestive growth, respiratory resilience, and pediatric skin care from infancy through adolescence.

## 2. Core Pediatric Focus Areas
- **Recurrent Respiratory & Allergic Conditions:** Supporting children prone to recurrent cough, tonsillitis, adenoid hypertrophy, and seasonal wheezing by strengthening *Pranavaha Srotas* and clearing *Kapha–Ama*.
- **Digestive Growth & Appetite Regulation (*Bala Agni*):** Addressing picky eating, malabsorption, infantile colic, chronic constipation, and nutritional growth faltering.
- **Pediatric Dermatology (Infantile Eczema / *Karappan*):** Gentle external care using GMP-certified [Chemparathyadi Keram](../medicines/02-chemparathyadi-keram.md) and blood-cooling (*Rakta Prasadana*) internal protocols.
- **Immunity & Cognitive Nourishment (*Swarnaprashana* & *Medhya Rasayana*):** Age-appropriate classical formulations including [Chyavanaprasam](../medicines/03-chyavanaprasam.md) to support school-year immunity and concentration.
