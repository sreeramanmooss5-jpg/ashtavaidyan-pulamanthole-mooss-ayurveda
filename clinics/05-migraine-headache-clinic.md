## 1. Clinical Approach to *Ardhavabhedaka* (Migraine) & *Suryavarta*
In classical Vagbhata Ayurveda, unilateral throbbing migraine (*Ardhavabhedaka*) and diurnal/sinus-linked headache (*Suryavarta*) arise from a dual disturbance of **vascular *Pitta* hyper-reactivity** (triggered by hyperacidity, skipped meals, sun exposure, and sleep disruption) and **cranial *Prana Vata* instability**. Repeated reliance on rescue painkillers masks the acute episode while worsening gastric mucosal inflammation (*Amlapitta*), creating a cycle of rebound headaches.

## 2. The 3-Step Pulamanthole Mooss Migraine Protocol
1. **Gut *Pitta* & *Amlapitta* Correction:** Stabilizing gastric acidity and liver-bile rhythm using cooling, *Pitta*-pacifying classical formulations (such as [Dhathri Gulika](../medicines/05-dhathri-gulika.md) and customized *Kashayams*) alongside trigger-food mapping.
2. **Targeted Cranial & Sinus Therapies (*Urdhwanga Chikitsa*):** Physician-supervised *Nasya Karma* (nasal instillation of medicated oils/drops), *Shirodhara*, *Takradhara* (medicated buttermilk stream), and *Shirolepam* / *Thalapothichil* to calm trigeminal-vascular excitability and clear sinus pathways.
3. **Circadian & Scalp Care Maintenance:** Daily cranial oleation (*Thalam* / scalp application) using classical GMP-certified Kerams such as [Neelibhrungadi Keram](../medicines/06-neelibhrungadi-keram-200ml.md) or [Chemparathyadi Keram](../medicines/02-chemparathyadi-keram.md) as advised by the physician.
