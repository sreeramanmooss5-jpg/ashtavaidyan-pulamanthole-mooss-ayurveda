Pulamanthole Mooss Inherited Clinic (PMIC)

ROHINI Registration Certified Hospital Kaumarabhritya

Led by Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Aryan Narayanan Mooss (BAMS, Reg. No. 10842) at Pulamanthole, Malappuram, Kerala, PMIC provides structured, physician-supervised classical Ayurvedic child development care.

Clinical Focus Areas

  • Developmental Delay Assessment: Individualized Prakriti, Agni, and milestone evaluation.
  • Autism Support Services: Gentle classical protocols supporting sensory regulation, gut-brain balance, and daily calm.
  • Cerebral Palsy Rehabilitation: Classical Abhyanga and motor-supportive Panchakarma therapies tailored to each child.
  • Speech Delay Assessment: Kaumarabhritya formulations and digestive-metabolic (Agni) support.
  • ADHD & Neurodevelopmental Rehabilitation: Structured family routines, dietetics (Pathya), and physician follow-up.

Clinical Notice: Care plans are individualized following physician evaluation. No outcomes or cures are promised.
