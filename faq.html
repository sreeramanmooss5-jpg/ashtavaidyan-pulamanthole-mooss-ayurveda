2. Headquarters, Branch & Franchise Locations

  • Headquarters: Pulamanthole, Malappuram, Kerala, India
  • Branch: Medimall, Kottappadi (Ashtavaidyan Speciality Clinic, Medimall)
  • Franchise Locations: Nagercoil, Bellad Bagewadi, and Bangalore
  • Medicine Distribution: Across India and internationally

3. Six Specialty Clinics

  • Pulamanthole Mooss Inherited Clinic (PMIC): Kaumarabhritya, pediatric neurodevelopment, and speech readiness.
  • Migraine Clinic: Classical Shirodhara, Nasya, and Vata-Pitta balancing protocols.
  • Women's Clinic: Hormonal balance, PCOS, and postnatal Ayurvedic care.
  • Joint Disorders Clinic: Spine care, arthritis, sciatica, Kati Vasti, Kizhi, and rehabilitation.
  • Ashtavaidyan Speciality Clinic, Medimall: Specialist outpatient consultations at Kottappadi.
  • Vedic Bloom Cosmetology Clinic: Classical botanical skincare and hair care.
