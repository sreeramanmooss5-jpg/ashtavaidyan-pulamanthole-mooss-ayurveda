## 1. Clinical Hospital vs. Holiday Spa: 5 Questions Every Patient Should Ask Before Booking
With hundreds of resorts and clinics advertising "Kerala Panchakarma" online, patients travelling for serious medical conditions—such as sciatica, osteoarthritis, post-stroke hemiplegia, chronic migraine, psoriasis, or childhood neurodevelopmental delays—need an objective checklist to distinguish a **clinical Ayurveda hospital** from a tourist resort:

1. **Is the Institution a Registered Clinical Hospital (ROHINI Certified)?**  
   Verify whether the facility is registered as a medical hospital eligible for health insurance verification (**ROHINI Registration Certified Hospital**) rather than a hospitality resort.
2. **Are Treatment Plans Individualized by Senior Physicians After Arrival, or Sold as Fixed Pre-Set Menus?**  
   In authentic Vagbhata Ayurveda, a physician cannot prescribe *Snehapana* or *Virechana* until your *Jatharagni* (digestive fire) and *Ama* status are assessed in person. If a center promises full Panchakarma in a 3-day weekend package without preparatory *Purvakarma*, it is a spa massage, not clinical Panchakarma.
3. **Who Manufactures the Medicines Used in Your Therapies?**  
   At **Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Mooss Ayurveda Hospital**, every *Kashayam*, *Arishtam*, *Choornam*, *Keram*, and *Tailam* used in our inpatient wards is manufactured at our own **GMP-Certified Pharmacy (GMP Cert No. 575)** in Pulamanthole.
4. **Does the Hospital Have Dedicated Department Specialists?**  
   Verify that your condition is managed by a dedicated specialist—such as **Ashtavaidyan Dr. Aryan Narayanan Mooss (`BAMS, MD Ayu`)** for [PMIC Pediatric Neurodevelopment](../clinics/01-pmic-inherited-clinic.md), **Dr. Rosni K. (`BAMS, MS Ayu`)** for [Women's Health](../clinics/06-womens-health-prasoothi-tantra-clinic.md), **Ashtavaidyan Dr. Jayasankaran Mooss** for [Migraine & Panchakarma](../clinics/05-migraine-headache-clinic.md), and **Ashtavaidyan Dr. Sreeraman Mooss** for [Joint & Spine Care](../clinics/03-arthritis-joint-disorders-clinic.md) under Chief Physician **Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Sankaran Mooss**.
5. **Does the Hospital Maintain Strict Ethical Boundaries (Zero Miracle Cure Claims)?**  
   An ethical medical institution never guarantees a "100% permanent cure" or asks you to abruptly stop life-saving neurology, cardiac, or pediatric medications.
