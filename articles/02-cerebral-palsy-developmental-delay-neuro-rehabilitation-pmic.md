## 1. Understanding Cerebral Palsy & Motor Delay Through Classical *Vata Vyadhi* & *Kaumarabhritya*
In classical Ayurvedic pediatrics, Cerebral Palsy (CP) and Global Developmental Delay (GDD) are evaluated through the lens of *Shiromarma Abhighataja Vata Vyadhi* (neurological *Vata* imbalance affecting cranial-motor pathways, muscle tone, and tendon elasticity). Depending on the presentation:
- **Spastic Cerebral Palsy (*Kuni / Pangulya / Stambha*):** Characterized by hypertonia, scissoring gait, tendon tightness (*Snayu Sankocham*), and *Ruksha Vata* predominance.
- **Hypotonic / Flaccid Developmental Delay (*Dhatu Kshaya / Mamsa Shaithilya*):** Characterized by poor head control, delayed sitting/standing, and weak truncal core stability.

## 2. How Inpatient PMIC Therapies Support Functional Motor Milestones
At the **Pulamanthole Mooss Inherited Clinic (PMIC)**, children undergo staged residential rehabilitation cycles combined with physiotherapy:
1. **Spasticity Softening (*Snehana* & *Ushma Sweda*):** Daily synchronized warm oil application using classical [Dhanwantharam Thailam](../medicines/11-dhanwantharam-thailam.md) and *Kayaseka (Pizhichil)* to reduce neuromuscular hyper-excitability and improve passive joint range of motion.
2. **Neuromuscular Nourishment (*Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda / Njavarakizhi*):** Warm boluses of medicinal *Njavara* rice cooked in *Bala* root decoction and cow's milk are massaged rhythmically along weak muscle groups to nourish *Mamsa* and *Majja Dhatu* (muscle and neural tissue).
3. **Pediatric *Matra Vasti* & Cranial *Shiro Pichu*:** Age-calibrated medicated lipid enemas (*Matra Vasti*) and cranial oleation to regulate systemic *Vata* and improve sleep, drooling control, and neck/trunk stability.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Block)
**Q1: How long is a typical inpatient rehabilitation cycle for Cerebral Palsy at PMIC?**  
Inpatient pediatric cycles at PMIC typically range from **14 to 28 days** depending on the child's age, spasticity level, and digestive tolerance, followed by a structured home-care protocol and periodic follow-up cycles every 4 to 6 months.

**Q2: Does PMIC provide accommodation for parents during a child's inpatient treatment?**  
Yes. All PMIC inpatient stays at our Pulamanthole riverside campus are family-centric, with dedicated Economy and Premium AC/Non-AC rooms where parents stay with the child and receive hands-on training in home *Abhyanga* and dietary care.
