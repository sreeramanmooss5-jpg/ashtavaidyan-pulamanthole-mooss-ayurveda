## 1. Why Sciatica (*Gridhrasi*) Persists When Only Pain Pathways Are Blocked
In Acharya Vagbhata's *Ashtanga Hridaya*, **Gridhrasi** (Sciatica) is described as a condition in which aggravated *Vata* (alone or combined with *Kapha*) compromises the major *Kandara* (nerve-tendon bundle) running from the lumbar spine (*Kati*) and hip (*Sphik*) down the posterior thigh, knee, calf, and foot.

When an intervertebral disc bulges at L4–L5 or L5–S1, three physiological factors sustain the pain:
1. **Paraspinal Muscle Guarding & Spasm (*Stambha*):** Tight lumbar muscles compress the affected disc space further.
2. **Perineural Inflammation (*Shopha*):** Localized swelling around the nerve root triggers radiating paresthesia (*Toda*, numbness, tingling).
3. ***Apana Vata* Stagnation in the Pelvic-Lumbar Basin:** Constipation, pelvic congestion, and prolonged sitting aggravate *Apana Vata* in its primary seat (*Pakwashaya / Kati*).

- **Step 2 — Decompressing *Apana Vata* via Classical *Vasti Karma*:** In classical Ayurveda, *Vasti* (medicated decoction and lipid enemas, such as *Erandamooladi Niruha Vasti* alternating with *Anuvasana / Matra Vasti*) is the definitive therapy for lumbar and sciatic nerve disorders because it acts directly at the anatomical seat of *Apana Vata*.
- **Step 3 — Paraspinal Nourishment (*Njavarakizhi*) & Ergonomic Prevention:** Strengthening paravertebral musculature to prevent recurrence after discharge.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Block)
**Q1: When does a disc bulge or sciatica require urgent surgical evaluation vs. conservative Ayurvedic spine care?**  
Most mild-to-moderate lumbar disc bulges (L4–L5, L5–S1) and sciatica respond well to structured conservative inpatient Panchakarma and rest. However, red-flag symptoms—such as sudden loss of bladder or bowel control (cauda equina syndrome) or rapidly progressive foot drop—require immediate neurosurgical evaluation. Our physicians review your lumbar MRI before admission to confirm safe conservative eligibility.
