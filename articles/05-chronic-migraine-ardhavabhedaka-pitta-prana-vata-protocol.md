## 1. Why Migraine Begins in the Gut–Vascular Axis (*Amlapitta* + *Prana Vata*)
In the *Uttarasthana* (*Shiro Roga Pratishedha*) of Vagbhata's *Ashtanga Hridaya*—elucidated in the Pulamanthole Mooss family's classical *Kairali* commentary—unilateral throbbing migraine (***Ardhavabhedaka***) and sunrise-to-noon escalating headache (***Suryavarta***) are driven by two linked mechanisms:
1. **Systemic *Pitta* & *Rakta* Hyper-Reactivity:** Triggered by skipped meals, fermented/spicy foods, excessive caffeine, late-night screen exposure, and unaddressed hyperacidity (*Amlapitta*).
2. **Cranial *Prana Vata* & *Kapha* Obstruction:** Cold wind exposure, sinus congestion, or cervical tension destabilizes *Prana Vata* in the cranial vessels (*Shiro Srotas*).

When patients take frequent NSAIDs or triptans without correcting gastric *Pitta*, medication-induced gastritis further aggravates *Amlapitta*—leading to **medication-overuse (rebound) headaches**.

## 2. The 3-Pillar Migraine Protocol Under Ashtavaidyan Dr. Jayasankaran Mooss
1. **Digestive *Pitta* & *Virechana* Reset:** Pacifying gastric acidity and biliary heat using cooling classical formulations (including [Dhathri Gulika](../medicines/05-dhathri-gulika.md) and *Pathyakshadhatryadi Kashayam*) and periodic therapeutic purgation (*Virechana*).
2. ***Nasya Karma* (Trans-Nasal Cranial Therapy):** In classical Ayurveda, *"Nasa hi shiraso dwaram"*—the nasal passages are the direct gateway to the cranial structures. Administering measured drops of classical medicated oils or herbal juices via *Nasya* clears sinus-vascular congestion and stabilizes trigeminal *Prana Vata*.
3. ***Takradhara*, *Shirolepam* & Daily Scalp Protection:** Medicated buttermilk stream therapy (*Takradhara*) and cooling herbal cranial paste (*Thalapothichil*) calm neuro-vascular hyper-excitability, supported at home by physician-selected scalp oils such as [Neelibhrungadi Keram](../medicines/06-neelibhrungadi-keram-200ml.md).

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Block)
**Q1: How does Ayurvedic treatment differentiate between Migraine (Ardhavabhedaka) and Sinus Headache (Suryavarta)?**  
*Ardhavabhedaka* typically presents as unilateral, pulsating pain with nausea, photophobia, and gastric *Pitta* triggers, whereas *Suryavarta* presents as frontal/periorbital headache that intensifies with the rising sun, peaks at midday, and subsides by evening due to combined *Vata–Pitta–Kapha* sinus congestion.
