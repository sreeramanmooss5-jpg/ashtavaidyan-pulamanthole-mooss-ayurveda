## 1. Clinical Specialization in Ayurvedic Gynecology, Maternal Health & Healthspan
**Dr. Rosni K. (BAMS, MS Ayu)** is a postgraduate specialist in ***Prasoothi Tantra & Sthree Roga*** (Ayurvedic Obstetrics & Gynecology) with over a decade of clinical experience. ## 2. Core Clinical Focus Areas
- **Menstrual & Hormonal Disorders (*Artava Vyapad*):** Root-cause management of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), irregular menstruation, dysmenorrhea (*Kashtartava*), endometriosis support, fibroids, and abnormal uterine bleeding (*Asrigdara*) through *Agni* correction, *Apana Vata* regulation, and specialized local procedures (*Uttara Vasti*, *Yoni Pichu*, *Yoni Prakshalana*).
- **Pre-Conception & Maternal Care (*Garbhadhana & Garbhini Paricharya*):** Structured detoxification and reproductive nourishment for couples planning conception, alongside month-wise prenatal care.
- **Classical Kerala Postnatal Care (*Sutika Paricharya*):** Comprehensive postpartum recovery protocols featuring [Dhanwantharam Thailam](../medicines/11-dhanwantharam-thailam.md) *Abhyanga*, herbal bath (*Vethukuli*), lactation support, and [Dasamoolarishtam](../medicines/04-dasamoolarishtam.md) to restore pelvic and spinal strength.
- **Circadian Healthspan & Botanical Dermatology (Vedic Bloom):** Supporting perimenopausal transition, *Rakta Shuddhi* skin clarity, and non-chemical skincare using [Pulamanthole Mooss Facial Powder (20g)](../medicines/08-pulamanthole-mooss-facial-powder-20gm.md).
