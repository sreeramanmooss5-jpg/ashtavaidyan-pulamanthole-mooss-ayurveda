## 1. Clinical Specialization in Pediatric Neurodevelopment & *Kaumarabhritya*
**Ashtavaidyan Dr. Aryan Narayanan Mooss (BAMS, MD Ayu)** leads the **Pulamanthole Mooss Inherited Clinic (PMIC)** and the Department of Pediatric Ayurveda (*Kaumarabhritya*). Combining postgraduate specialization in Ayurveda with thePulamanthole Ashtavaidya family's *Uttarasthana* (*Bala Chikitsa*) heritage, he pioneered PMIC's family-centric, long-duration developmental support model.

## 2. Core Pediatric & Neurodevelopmental Expertise
- **Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) & Sensory Regulation:** Addressing the *Jatharagni–Srotas–Ojas* gut–brain axis, bowel regularity, sleep architecture, and *Prana/Udana Vata* balance in children with ASD.
- **Cerebral Palsy (CP) & Motor Milestones:** Staged *Abhyanga*, *Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda (Njavarakizhi)*, and pediatric *Matra Vasti* protocols to support muscle tone, reduce spasticity, and improve functional motor milestones alongside physiotherapy.
- **Speech Delay, Global Developmental Delay (GDD) & ADHD:** Non-sedating neuro-sensory calming (*Shiro Pichu*, *Thalapothichil*, *Takradhara*) and *Medhya* nutritional support.
- **General Pediatric Immunity & Dermatology:** Managing recurrent childhood respiratory allergies, digestive growth, and infantile eczema (*Karappan*).
