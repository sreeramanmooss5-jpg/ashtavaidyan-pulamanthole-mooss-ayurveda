## 1. Clinical Specialization & Leadership
**Ashtavaidyan Dr. Sreeraman Mooss** serves as Chief Physician, Healthcare Administrator, and Head of the **[Arthritis & Joint Disorders Specialty Clinic](../clinics/03-arthritis-joint-disorders-clinic.md)** and **[Back Pain & Spine Care Clinic](../clinics/04-back-pain-spine-care-clinic.md)** at Ashtavaidyan Pulamanthole Mooss Ayurveda Hospital. Bridging multi-generational Ashtavaidya diagnostic scholarship with structured clinical protocols and GMP pharmaceutical quality systems, he also directs national physician education through *"The Pulamanthole Prescriptions"* and leads the **Pulamanthole Mooss Ayurveda Thanal Foundation** (`CIN: U86201KL2025NPL092515`).

## 2. Core Clinical Focus Areas
- **Arthritis & Inflammatory/Degenerative Joint Disorders:** Differential diagnosis and staged management of *Sandhigata Vata* (Osteoarthritis), *Amavata* (Rheumatoid Arthritis), *Vatarakta* (Gout), and *Avabahuka* (Frozen Shoulder) using classical *Dhanyamla Dhara*, *Upanaha*, *Janu Vasti*, and *Tikta Ksheera Vasti*.
- **Non-Surgical Spine & Sciatica Rehabilitation:** Root-cause *Apana Vata* and intervertebral disc care for *Gridhrasi* (Sciatica), lumbar/cervical spondylosis, and disc prolapse.
- **Digestive *Jatharagni* & Preventive Longevity:** Educating patients and clinicians on how *Mandagni* (sluggish digestive fire) and *Ama* accumulation precede systemic musculoskeletal inflammation.
